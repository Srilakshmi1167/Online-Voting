Welcome to Online Voting Portal

The Digital Voting System is a secure and modern platform designed for conducting fair elections in educational institutions.

It ensures transparency, accuracy, and integrity in the voting process. Each user is allowed to vote only once, preventing duplicate or fake votes.

The system is built to be fast, reliable, and accessible from anywhere, making elections easy and efficient.
